What should I do before listing my home for sale?

By Alecia Jones · October 21, 2026

The homes that sell fastest and for the most money usually have three things in common: the right price, a clean and simple look, and great photos.

Price it right from day one. Overpricing is the most common mistake I see. Homes that sit get passed over, and the price usually ends up lower than if it had been priced correctly at the start.

Fix the small things. Leaky faucets, burned-out bulbs, chipped paint, and sticky doors make buyers wonder what else was ignored. A weekend of small repairs pays off.

Clear the clutter. Pack away personal photos, extra furniture, and anything on the counters. Buyers need to picture their own life in the space.

Think about curb appeal. In Southern Utah, fresh rock, trimmed plants, and a clean entry go a long way. It’s the first thing buyers see online and in person.
